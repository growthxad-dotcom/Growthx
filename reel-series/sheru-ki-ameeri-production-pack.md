# SHERU KI AMEERI — Reel Series (Part 1 + Part 2)

Format: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, har part ~60-70 sec, 12 scenes (har scene ~5 sec).
Style: photoreal anthropomorphic jaanwar, Indian setting, golden-hour warm light, rich gold/ivory colour palette, har 4-5 sec mein cut.
Tools: Image -> Midjourney / Leonardo / Flux. Image-to-video -> Kling / Hailuo / Veo. Voice -> ElevenLabs (Hindi male, deep, emotional). Edit -> CapCut.

---

## 1. KAHANI (viral hook)

**Title:** Garib samajh kar shaadi se nikala... phir aasmaan se SONA barsa

**Core idea:** Sheru ek safed sher hai jo asal mein "Sher Empire" ka secret malik hai (hazaron crore ki company, sone ki khaanein, private jets). Wo simple kapdon mein, purani cycle pe apni behen ki beti (bhanji) Simmi ki shaadi mein jaata hai, kyunki wo dekhna chahta hai ki rishtedaar uski izzat paise se karte hain ya dil se. Ameer rishtedaar use "bhikhari" samajh kar bahar nikalte hain. Sirf bhanji uska haath pakadti hai.

**Moral:** Insaan ki keemat uske kapdon se nahi, uske kirdaar se hoti hai.

**Viral triggers:** beizzati -> sacha pyaar -> cliffhanger -> "Part 2 mein dekho" -> extreme ameeri reveal (helicopters se sona, sone ki Rolls-Royce, bride ko sone mein tolna).

**Series hook:** Part 1 ka last frame = aasmaan mein 100 helicopters ki awaaz. Caption: "Part 2 mein dekho Sheru ne kya kiya..."

## 2. CHARACTER BIBLE (har prompt mein copy-paste karo, consistency ke liye)

- **[SHERU-POOR]**: white-maned male lion, anthropomorphic, plain faded kurta pajama, torn cloth bag, old rusty bicycle, humble kind eyes, dusty feet in chappals.
- **[SHERU-RICH]**: same white-maned male lion, ivory-and-gold embroidered sherwani with ruby buttons, gold lion-head chain, diamond watch, white sneakers with gold lion logo, sunglasses, regal and calm.
- **[SIMMI]**: young lioness bride, light golden fur, red bridal lehenga, kind eyes, gold jewellery.
- **[CHACHA-SETH]**: fat arrogant bull in silk kurta, thick gold chains, cigar-less, sneering face.
- **[MAAMI-MOR]**: peacock woman in blue-silver designer lehenga, heavy diamond necklace, gossiping.
- **[HIPPO-GANG]**: 3 hippo/rhino/hyena aunties in heavy silk sarees, laughing mockingly.
- **[GUARD]**: black-suit security guard, gorilla.
- **[MUNIM-BANDAR]**: monkey in black waistcoat with notepad and round glasses (Sheru's assistant).

Style suffix for every image prompt:
`hyper-realistic anthropomorphic animals, cinematic 8k, shallow depth of field, golden hour light, rich Indian wedding/village setting, Pixar-meets-photoreal fur detail, vertical 9:16 --ar 9:16`

Logo: apna channel logo neeche right corner mein (CapCut overlay), chhota aur semi-transparent.
