## GLOBAL BRANDING & EDITING GUIDELINES

### 1. Color palette
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Main Orange | `#F59E0B` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |
| Black | `#101010` |
| Charcoal | `#1C1C1C` |

Orange only for important highlights, graphics, lines and headings. White for readable information. Black and charcoal for primary backgrounds.

### 2. 2D animation style
Flat 2D illustrations; smooth infographic animations; animated typography; vector-style icons; candlestick and chart animations; clean diagrams and data visualizations. No unnecessary 3D cartoon mascot.

### 3. Real photos and footage
Use high-quality, genuine, licensed stock of: traders at computers, financial market monitors, smartphones and laptops, people learning financial concepts, office/workstation environments, realistic technology and AI visuals. **No fabricated profit screenshots or fake testimonials.**

### 4. Transitions and effects
Smooth zoom in/out, motion blur, whip pan, mask transitions, light sweep, parallax, speed ramp, kinetic typography, subtle glitch, animated arrows and lines. Match effects to the scene; do not overload.

### 5. Captions
- Every voiceover sentence has readable subtitles.
- Bold white text; important words highlighted in orange.
- Keep clear of Instagram's bottom interface.
- Max 2 lines at a time.
- Same font across all reels.

### 6. Background music
Each reel gets its own track suited to its subject:
- Educational: minimal electronic
- AI: futuristic technology
- Mistakes and risks: suspenseful
- Psychology: emotional cinematic
- Community: inspirational
- Comparisons: modern corporate

Royalty-free or properly licensed only. Keep below the voiceover.

### 7. Final end screen (2 seconds, every reel)
```
TRADEWITHAI
@_tradewithai
FOLLOW FOR TRADING EDUCATION
LIKE | COMMENT | SHARE
```
Black background · centered logo · orange animated underline · white Instagram handle.

### 8. Instagram caption rule
- Mention @_tradewithai in every caption.
- Short, engaging first line.
- One question to prompt comments.
- 4–5 relevant hashtags.
- Never claim guaranteed profit.
- Add a risk disclaimer on trading-related posts.

### 9. PDF delivery rule
When a reel promises a PDF, prepare that specific educational PDF **before** inviting comments. Deliver the promised resource accurately; do not imply that commenting automatically generates a document.

### 10. Financial safety
Trading involves substantial financial risk. Never promise returns, guaranteed signals or easy income. Quotex / digital-options content must not encourage deposits or sign-ups unless the platform's current legal and regulatory position in India has been independently verified.
